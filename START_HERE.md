## How to Test the Real App

### Step 1: Open the Real App
Go to: **http://localhost:8000/index.html**

NOT debug.html - that's just a testing tool.

### Step 2: What You Should See
- Black background
- Modal dialog with "👤 Nueva Identidad"
- Green box with wallet address (0x...)
- Input field for your name
- Green "Continuar" button

### Step 3: Create Your User
1. Look at the wallet address in the green box
2. Enter your name (e.g., "Juan")
3. Click "Continuar"
4. You should see the home screen

### Step 4: Verify It Works
- Click ⚙️ settings button
- You should see your full wallet address
- Click 📋 to copy it
- You should see "✅ Copiada"
